# Standard deliverable template

Every lead-agent synthesis uses this structure. Keep each section concise and
evidence-led. Omit a section only with an explicit note on why.

## 1. Problem definition
What customer or business problem this addresses, and for whom.

## 2. Evidence considered
Sources reviewed, graded (Observed / Reported / Inferred / Assumed).

## 3. Assumptions and unknowns
What we are assuming, and the highest-risk unknowns.

## 4. Recommended approach
The single strongest recommendation, stated first with rationale.

## 5. Alternatives considered
Options weighed and why they were not chosen.

## 6. Accessibility implications
Barriers, required behaviour, and the compliance caveat.

## 7. Design-system implications
Reuse, proposed divergence, and governance/migration notes.

## 8. Success measures
Primary metric, secondary and guardrail metrics.

## 9. Validation plan
Lightest proportionate way to reduce the biggest uncertainty.
