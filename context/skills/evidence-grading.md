# Skill: Evidence grading

A shared taxonomy so every role grades evidence the same way. Referenced by
`../context/HOUSE_STYLE.md`.

## When to use

- Whenever a claim informs a recommendation.
- When summarising research, analytics or stakeholder input.

## Grades

- **Observed** — directly supported by research or behavioural data.
- **Reported** — stated by participants, customers or stakeholders.
- **Inferred** — a reasonable interpretation that still needs validation.
- **Assumed** — unsupported; requires investigation.

## Procedure

1. List each claim relied upon.
2. Assign one grade per claim.
3. Note source and any limitation (sample size, market, recency, device).
4. Flag contradictions; do not average them away.
5. Identify the highest-risk assumption for the decision at hand.

## Output format

| Claim | Grade | Source | Limitation |
|-------|-------|--------|------------|
| Users abandon at step 3 | Observed | Funnel analytics, last 90d | Desktop only |
| They find it confusing | Reported | 5 interviews | Small sample |

Follow with:
- **Highest-risk assumption:** …
- **Recommended validation:** lightest method to reduce that risk.
